# UGH Tails EP 001 "THE GATE" — animation fixes from the first external review

**Source:** Shayne Perryman, 2026-09-27, unsolicited review of
`https://www.youtube.com/shorts/BywyeiL6e-c`. First outside critique UGH has ever had.
Not a friend, not a peer session — a stranger who started uninterested.

**Owner: PRODUCER (`peer-morning-run` — video lane).** The Copywriter wrote this spec and does
not touch the build. No copy changes here; narration and on-screen lines stay exactly as they are.

---

## DO NOT CHANGE — these landed, and a re-render must preserve them

- **Art style and character design.** Read to him as "a more modern and refined *Lady and the
  Tramp*." This is the thing that converted him. Do not restyle.
- **Mabel's expressions as she opens the gate.** Called out by name as the standout beat.
- **Pip examining the map at the top.** He named this as the smoothest, best-flowing animation in
  the piece. **Use it as the internal quality bar** — every other shot should move like this one.
- The human-like expressions and poses. He specifically said they do *not* break immersion and
  read as cute. Earlier worry that they were too anthropomorphic is answered: leave them.

## FIX — five defects, in severity order

1. **Red thought bubble over Tank is cropped.** The bottom of the bubble is sliced off by the
   frame. Straight rendering bug, and it is the one that most makes the piece look unfinished.

2. **The UGH beat is a freeze-frame.** At the midpoint, where "UGH" appears and the speech bubbles
   fade in, the picture holds completely still. He described it as looking "like a presentation."
   Add continuous subtle motion *under* the bubble fade — a slight head bob, a drift or ruffle in
   the background. The bubbles can still fade; the frame must not stop.

3. **Loop seams are visible.** The gate-pushing loops show a distinct stop before the loop
   restarts. His diagnosis is the important part: when it hitches, it **falls out of sync with the
   narration and the VO stops sounding like storytelling and starts sounding like description.**
   Fix with a cross-fade or ping-pong loop, or extend the shot so it does not need to cycle.

4. **Tank's flex reads as pink smoke.** When Tank flexes he opens his mouth and something pink
   emerges that looks like exhaled smoke. It confused him. Re-render the shot or cut the mouth
   opening.

5. **Mabel never returns to sitting.** Her hold ends mid-pose. Animate the return so the shot
   resolves.

## Proof required before this is called done

Per charter §4: state the outcome first, run the real path, capture evidence, show it can fail.
Specifically — a frame grab of the Tank thought bubble showing the full rounded bottom inside the
frame, and the UGH beat exported and watched to confirm the picture never fully stops.
