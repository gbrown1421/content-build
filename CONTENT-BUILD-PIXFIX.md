## The PixFix voice — read once, apply everywhere

**Studio craftsman. Plain, precise, quietly proud of the work.** Sentence case. Short
declarative sentences. No hype words — no "amazing," "stunning," "game-changer." No emoji.
The work is the proof; the copy says what it is and gets out of the way.

**Never UGH voice on a PixFix post.** No lowercase-muttered captions, no pathway sign-offs,
no "ugh. we show up." PixFix is a different company to a different buyer.

**Every CTA caption carries both halves of the link rule:** the raw URL (Facebook makes it
clickable) **and** "link in bio" (Instagram never does). `post-ghl.js` refuses a caption
missing either one.

**Links:** `pixfixstudio.com` · order page `pixfixstudio.com/order.html`. Nothing else.

**Facts you may state — and only these:**
- One image in; **8 angles** back: front, left profile, right profile, three-quarter left,
  three-quarter right, rear, rear three-quarter left, rear three-quarter right
- Plus **one turnaround plate** (all eight on one sheet) → **9 files for one character**
- A cast of two or more also gets **a size chart**
- Finals are **transparent PNGs**, named per angle, no watermark
- **From $129.** Standard 5 days · Priority 3 days · Rush 24 hours

**Never state or imply a customer, winner, testimonial or review that does not exist.**
