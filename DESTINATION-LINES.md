## Aruba
- **Fall:** Aruba sits below the hurricane belt. Fall there is dry, breezy and warm, and the beaches are quieter before winter.
- **Winter:** Winter is Aruba's high season. Dry, breezy, mid-eighties, and it barely rains.
- **Spring:** Aruba stays dry and breezy into spring. The winter crowds thin and the water stays warm.

## Boston
- **Fall:** Boston is at its best in the fall. Crisp, walkable, and New England color peaks in October.
- **Winter:** Boston goes indoors in winter. Museums, oyster bars and North End kitchens carry the season.
- **Spring:** Boston wakes up in spring. Cool mornings, long light, and the Public Garden in bloom by May.

## Cancún
- **Fall:** Cancún gets quieter in the fall. The rain eases by late November and the water stays warm.
- **Winter:** Winter is Cancún's best weather. Dry, sunny, low eighties, and the sea is calm and clear.
- **Spring:** Cancún is hot and dry in spring. March has the spring-break crowds; April and May are calmer.

## Charleston
- **Fall:** Charleston cools off in the fall. The humidity breaks, the evenings stay warm, and it's the best walking weather of the year.
- **Winter:** Charleston is mild and quiet in winter. High fifties, short lines, and peak oyster season.
- **Spring:** Charleston is at its prettiest in spring. Azaleas, warm afternoons, and every patio open again.

## Chicago
- **Fall:** Chicago is cool and clear in the fall. The lakefront is at its best, and so is the architecture.
- **Winter:** Chicago goes indoors in winter. Museums, jazz clubs and deep-dish carry the cold months.
- **Spring:** Chicago warms up slowly, then all at once. Patios open and the lakefront path fills back up.

## Denver
- **Fall:** Denver is dry and sunny in the fall. The aspens in the mountains nearby go gold.
- **Winter:** Denver is sunnier in winter than people expect. The ski resorts are an hour or two away.
- **Spring:** Denver swings between sun and a late snow in spring. The mountains hold their snow into April.

## Dublin
- **Fall:** Dublin is cool and changeable in the fall. The summer crowds are gone and the pubs do the rest.
- **Winter:** Dublin has short days and long evenings in winter. That's what the pubs and the live music are for.
- **Spring:** Dublin turns green in spring. Longer days, milder air, and St. Patrick's in March.

## Fort Lauderdale

## Fort Myers
